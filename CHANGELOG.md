# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Until 1.0, a minor version
may change how things work.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

The first release.

- Floating islands along the top of every screen: the launcher button, workspaces, the focused window, a clock (24-hour or AM/PM, with or without seconds), the keyboard layout, the tray and the volume.
- Islands that plugins bring, on the left or on the right.
- Settings in `bar.toml`, and a page for them in the settings window.
